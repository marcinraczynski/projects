Approach

From understanding the environment to delivering concrete action

We build Public Affairs strategies that support business objectives, strengthen an organisation’s position in its institutional and social environment, and enable it to take an effective part in public debate.

We combine analytical, advisory and communication expertise.

  • Political & Regulatory Intelligence

    We go beyond legislative monitoring: we analyse business consequences, identify risks early and plan scenarios.

  • Social Licence to Operate

    We build public acceptance for operations and investments, particularly in energy, infrastructure, mining and transport.

  • Integrated Public Affairs

    We combine institutional advisory with strategic communication, reputation management and social dialogue across the whole of K+ Group.

K+ Group Public Affairs methodology

How we work

Four stages form a cycle. Monitoring closes one round and opens the next, because an organisation’s environment never stops changing.

Understand Strategize Engage Monitor & Adapt

Stage 02 / 04

Strategize

Goals, priorities, scenarios and an action strategy.

  1. 01Understand: Diagnosis of the business, its environment, stakeholders and risks.
  2. 02Strategize: Goals, priorities, scenarios and an action strategy.
  3. 03Engage: Dialogue, relationships, arguments and communication.
  4. 04Monitor & Adapt: Ongoing analysis of change, evaluation and updating of tactics.
